What am I seeing?
  • An eccentric load makes the bolt group rotate about its centroid. Every bolt then carries a share of the direct load plus a force at right angles to its radius, and the bolt furthest from the centroid works hardest.
  • A bolt can fail by shearing through, by crushing the plate behind it in bearing, or by tearing out a whole block of plate along the line of holes. The worst of these governs.
  • A bolted end plate in tension bends like a T. A thin plate yields in a double curvature with prying at its edges, a thick plate lets the bolts fail first. That is mode 1 against mode 3.
  • A fillet weld is about 22 per cent stronger loaded across its length than along it. The directional method uses the real angle at the worst point of the weld.
  • A base plate spreads the column load onto weaker concrete. Only a strip c wide around the steel profile really bears, and c grows with the plate thickness.
