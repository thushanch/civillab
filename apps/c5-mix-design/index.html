What am I seeing?
  • The method is ACI 211.1, absolute volume. Water comes from the slump and stone size, the water to cement ratio from the strength, the stone from its packing, and the sand fills whatever volume is left.
  • Strength is set almost entirely by the water to cement ratio. Drag the point along the curve and watch the strength fall as the paste gets wetter, which is Abrams' law.
  • The mix is designed for a mean strength above the characteristic one, because real batches scatter. A larger standard deviation pushes the target up and costs cement.
  • Durability can override strength. An aggressive exposure caps the water to cement ratio and sets a minimum cement content, even when strength alone would allow a leaner mix.
  • Wet aggregate brings its own water. The batch water is cut by the free moisture on the stockpiles so the paste the mixer makes is still the one designed.
