What am I seeing?
  • The whole web of phase relationships hangs off one identity: Se = wGs.
  • Void ratio e compares voids to solids; porosity n = e/(1+e) compares voids to the total.
  • Water content cannot exceed e/Gs, the value at full saturation.
  • Unit weights fall out of the block directly: γdry = Gsγw/(1+e) and γsat adds the water-filled voids.
