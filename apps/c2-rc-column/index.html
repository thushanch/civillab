What am I seeing?
  • Every point on the interaction curve is one neutral axis depth. Sweep x from zero to beyond the section and the strain diagram walks the section from pure tension, through pure bending, to pure squash.
  • The balanced point is where the concrete crushes just as the tension steel yields. Above it the steel never yields and extra axial load eats the moment capacity. Below it a little axial load actually helps.
  • Drag the design point. Inside the curve the column works, outside it fails. The capacity is read at the same axial load, so the moment is what gets checked.
  • A slender column bows under load, and the bow adds a moment N·e2 that a stocky column never sees. Watch MEd jump when λ passes λlim.
  • The imperfection here acts about the major axis only, as EN 1992-1-1 5.8.9 allows. The minor axis carries its own moment plus any second order moment.
