What am I seeing?
  • Natural frequency depends only on mass and stiffness: ωn = √(k/m). Damping barely changes it.
  • Damping does not change the shape of the motion, only how fast the envelope decays.
  • A building with n floors has n natural frequencies and n mode shapes.
  • In the first mode every floor moves the same way; higher modes contain sign reversals.
  • Press Play and watch the free vibration decay, or switch to a shear building to see the modes.
