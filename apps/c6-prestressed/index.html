What am I seeing?
  • Stresses here are compression positive, the usual prestressing convention. The tendon below the centroid squeezes the bottom fibre and lifts the top one, and the load does the opposite.
  • Two moments of truth. At transfer the force is at its largest and only the self weight helps, so the top cracks or the bottom crushes. In service the force has fallen and the full load acts, so the bottom goes into tension.
  • The Magnel diagram turns the four fibre limits into straight lines in eccentricity against one over the force. Any point inside the shaded region satisfies all of them at once. Drag the point and see which line it crosses.
  • The tendon loses force before the beam ever carries load. Friction and draw-in in the duct, the concrete shortening as it is squeezed, then shrinkage, creep and relaxation over the years. A fifth of the jacking force is a typical total.
  • The code toggle changes only the allowable stresses. The losses follow EN 1992-1-1 5.10 in both cases.
