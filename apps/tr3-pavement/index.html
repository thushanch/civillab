What am I seeing?
  • The tyre presses on a small circle, and the stress spreads out and falls with depth. By the time it reaches the soil it must be small enough for the soil to carry it for millions of axles.
  • Stiff layers spread the load fastest. Odemark turns each layer into an equivalent depth of soil, a stiff asphalt layer counting several times its real thickness.
  • The CBR of the subgrade drives the whole design. Halve it and the thickness needed jumps, because the weakest material sets how much cover it needs.
  • Each layer needs enough stronger material above it. That is why the CBR method is applied over the subgrade and again over the subbase.
  • The mechanistic check limits the vertical strain at the top of the subgrade, which controls rutting. Halving the strain multiplies the life by about 22.
