What am I seeing?
  • Timber is weaker the longer a load stays on it, and weaker again when it is wet. kmod carries both effects at once, which is why a roof under snow can take more than a floor under its own weight.
  • The chain runs from the characteristic strength through kmod, kh and ksys to the design value. Each box only appears when it changes something.
  • A deep narrow joist can twist sideways before it breaks. kcrit falls once the relative slenderness passes 0.75, and blocking or a nailed floor brings it back to one.
  • Timber is soft across the grain. A beam can pass bending and shear and still crush the timber where it sits on its support.
  • BS 5268 works with permissible stresses under working loads, so its K factors play the part of kmod and the safety margin hides inside the grade stresses.
